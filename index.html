Naver Blog & Cafe

Naver is the dominant search and content platform in Korea. Over the year I built a publishing rhythm that drove organic discovery and positioned the studio as a serious learning community — 230+ posts across expression lessons, member features, and studio storytelling.

Top 10 Performing Posts By lifetime views

All ten top performers are expression lessons — posts that teach one specific English phrase. Promotion-first posts averaged 5–15 views. The lesson format is 10–20× more effective.

Content Mix By post type

A three-layer strategy: expression lessons drive discovery, foreign friend profiles build community, and studio reflections build trust.

Key insight

Naver rewards specificity. Posts that solved one small, concrete problem for the reader — "how do you say 내 말이 in English?" — consistently outperformed posts about the studio itself. This shaped my editorial calendar: lead with use value, close with community.

What I Built

  • Publishing rhythm: 2–3 posts per week across expression lessons, member features, and studio updates
  • SEO-driven writing: keyword research for "영어회화," "강남 스터디," "AI 번역기," and related searches
  • Content series: recurring formats like Pop-Ref Series and 한국어에만 있는 감정 시리즈
  • Objection-first writing: direct responses to AI skepticism and study-group hesitancy
  • Community features: weekly foreign friend profiles (Alexa, Carmen, Pablo, and 40+ others)
