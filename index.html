Strategic Communication & International Systems Design

I architect communication systems and cross-cultural strategies for organizations operating across Europe and Asia.

With a foundation in international relations and interlingual mediation, I've worked across institutional, private, and non-profit sectors—from the European Parliament to Seoul-based startups. I speak five languages and have lived and worked in Italy, the UK, Luxembourg, and South Korea.

My work sits at the intersection of:

  • International communication & stakeholder engagement
  • UI/UX consultancy for multilingual platforms
  • Process automation & workflow design
  • Brand strategy & content architecture

I don't overcomplicate things. I sit with the problem, consult with people who know more, and build systems that are clear, human, and scalable.

Open to conversations about international communication, institutional strategy, and creative problem-solving.
